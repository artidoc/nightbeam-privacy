Privacy Policy

Nightbeam · effective date: 7 November 2026

1. General provisions

1.1. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) sets out what information is collected and how it is processed when you use the computer game “Nightbeam” (the “Game”).

1.2. The Game is developed by an independent team of developers (“we”, “us”, “our”).

1.3. This Policy applies to all versions of the Game published on game portals and websites. By using the Game, you confirm that you have read this Policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1. We collect only anonymised information about the use of the Game that does not allow you to be identified:

  • information about gameplay: the start and end of play sessions, progress achieved, choices made in the Game, playing time and decisions regarding offers to watch advertising;
  • general information about the conditions of use: interface language, device type (computer or mobile device), screen orientation and the game portal on which the Game is run;
  • the country from which the Game is accessed. The country is determined automatically from the network address; the network address itself is not stored by us.

3. Information we do not collect

3.1. We do not collect or process:

  • your name or any other information that identifies you;
  • your e-mail address, telephone number or other contact details;
  • account data from game portals or other services;
  • payment or banking information;
  • precise location information;
  • advertising identifiers, contact lists or other information stored on your device.

3.2. We do not link the information collected to any individual and do not create user profiles.

4. Purposes of processing

4.1. The information collected is used solely to:

  • analyse how the Game is used and improve its content, difficulty and balance;
  • identify and correct errors;
  • assess interest in the Game on different game portals.

4.2. The information is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not disclosed to third parties for marketing purposes.

4.3. For users in the European Union, the legal basis for processing is our legitimate interest in improving the Game (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

5. Disclosure to third parties

5.1. To process statistics we use the services of PostHog Inc., which acts on our behalf as a data processor. The information is stored and processed on servers located in the European Union.

5.2. The Game is published on game portals (for example CrazyGames, Yandex Games, Poki and others). The portals and their advertising partners process data in accordance with their own privacy policies, which you can read on the portals’ websites. Advertising in the Game is shown exclusively by means of the game portal.

5.3. The information is not disclosed to any other third party, except where expressly required by applicable law.

6. Retention

6.1. Anonymised information is kept for no longer than 25 (twenty-five) months from the date it is received and is then deleted.

6.2. Aggregated findings derived from it that contain no information about individual users may be kept for longer.

7. Cookies and game saves

7.1. The Game does not use cookies.

7.2. Game progress and settings are saved on your device or by means of the game portal solely to make the Game work. We do not collect this data. You may delete it by clearing your browser data or by using the tools provided by the game portal.

8. Your rights

8.1. You may at any time object to the processing, request information about how it is carried out, or ask any question concerning this Policy by writing to us at contact.creatives@proton.me.

8.2. As the information collected is anonymised, we are generally unable to attribute it to a specific user; in that case we will tell you so in our reply.

8.3. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority where you live.

9. Children

9.1. The Game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information about children. If you believe that a child has provided us with any information, please contact us at the address above.

10. Changes to this Policy

10.1. We may amend this Policy. The new version will be published on this page together with its effective date.

10.2. The current version takes effect on 7 November 2026.

3.1. Мы не собираем и не обрабатываем:

4. Цели обработки

4.1. Собранные сведения используются исключительно для:

  • анализа того, как используется Игра, и улучшения её содержания, сложности и баланса;
  • выявления и устранения ошибок;
  • оценки интереса к Игре на разных игровых площадках.

4.2. Сведения не используются для рекламы, не продаются и не передаются третьим лицам в целях маркетинга.

6.1. Обезличенные сведения хранятся не дольше 25 (двадцати пяти) месяцев с момента их получения, после чего удаляются.
